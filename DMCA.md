# DMCA & Content Policy

phase.rs is a non-commercial fan project built under the spirit of the
[Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy](https://company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy).
It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or approved by Wizards
of the Coast LLC or Hasbro, Inc.

This repository does not bundle MTG card images, card art, mana symbol
artwork, card-frame graphics, or the Comprehensive Rules document. Card
images and mana symbols are fetched from [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com/)
at runtime. Card metadata is sourced from [MTGJSON](https://mtgjson.com/).

Simplified-Chinese card *text* is sourced from
[magic-cards-zhs](https://github.com/HeliumOctahelide/magic-cards-zhs)
(CC BY-SA 4.0) and is redistributed in the `card-data.zh.json` content sidecar,
which is a derivative work of that data set. Simplified-Chinese card *images*
are fetched at runtime from [mtgch.com](https://mtgch.com/) when the user
selects that source. Neither upstream project makes any claim resolving the
underlying Wizards of the Coast copyright in the card text and images it
distributes, and neither does this project; the text sidecar is generated by
`scripts/gen-zhs-card-data.sh` and can be removed from a build, and the image
source is a user-facing setting. See [NOTICE](NOTICE).

## Reporting a concern

If you are a rights holder (or an authorized representative of one) and you
believe content in this repository, in the hosted application at
[phase-rs.dev](https://phase-rs.dev), or in a released build infringes your
rights, please contact the maintainer directly:

- **Email:** [matt@phase-rs.dev](mailto:matt@phase-rs.dev)

To help us respond quickly, please include:

1. A description of the copyrighted or trademarked work you believe is being
   infringed.
2. A link to the specific file, URL, commit, or asset in question.
3. Your contact information (email at minimum).
4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not
   authorized by the rights holder, an agent of the rights holder, or the law.
5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice
   is accurate and that you are the rights holder or authorized to act on
   their behalf.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

## Response

This project is maintained by one person in their spare time. Good-faith
reports will be acknowledged within a reasonable timeframe and substantive
requests will be addressed promptly. The maintainer is not a lawyer and will
generally prefer cooperative resolution over escalation.

You may also file a formal DMCA takedown with GitHub at
<https://github.com/contact/dmca>.
